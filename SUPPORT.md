# Support

Need help with Framio? Here's where to go.

- **A bug, a feature idea, or a museum to add** — open an issue with the right
  [template](https://github.com/pedrobritx/framio/issues/new/choose).
- **A question, or general feedback** — email **pedrobritx@gmail.com**.
- **Contributing** — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Getting art onto your devices** — see [docs/EXPORTS.md](docs/EXPORTS.md) and
  [docs/FRAME-TV.md](docs/FRAME-TV.md).

Please don't report security issues in public — see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
